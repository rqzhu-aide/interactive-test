# Evaluate Useful Consultation

For the fixture author and independent final reviewer, not the user actor.
Freeze criteria before execution. Review the complete objective from actual
conversation, disclosures, artifacts and available invocation evidence.
Use the private [observation profiles](observation-profiles.md) for the frozen
candidate's record and response contracts. Keep those mechanics out of actor
instructions and bundles; assess the actor from its own allowed input.

## Main Judgments

| Dimension | Ask | Evidence of a consequential problem |
|---|---|---|
| Investigation and proactivity | Did the consultant choose an attainable high-value question, source or review and use its answer? | An answerable, consequential fact the user was unlikely to volunteer is left unasked and affects the result; an uninformative review substitutes for the needed question |
| Scientific and computational quality | Do data meaning, preparation, target, identification, estimator, uncertainty and claims agree? | Wrong rows or dates, post-treatment adjustment, unsupported identification, incorrect computation, or silent target substitution |
| Continuity and user usefulness | Does the user receive understandable, actionable advice consistent with corrections and earlier evidence? | Old rejected advice remains current; the plain-language summary broadens the claim; requested feasible work never reaches a useful conclusion |
| Complete-objective effort | Was user effort and investigation proportionate to progress? | Repeated answered questions, requests for explicitly unavailable material, redundant reviews or unnecessary confirmation before clear work |

Trace the relevant chain: identified uncertainty, specialist finding when
needed, lead disposition, factual contribution/source, interpreted meaning, and
changed or explicitly preserved preparation, strategy or advice. Do not require
every link on every turn. Direct supported work, evidence-only synthesis and
useful stopping are positive controls, not elicitation failures.

For each material credited fact or alleged factual error, establish what was
disclosed or legitimately inspected by that turn and how the consultant used it.
Hidden world facts can expose actor drift but do not establish consultant
knowledge. An attachment release without evidence of inspection or a reviewer
packet alone cannot prove that the consultant learned a fact. Distinguish an
unasked question, a disclosed unknown, an established unavailable source and an
adopted assumption. Do not turn private unavailability into credit for a justified
stop. Judge an omitted question by its visible relevance and what an attainable
answer could change in the advice or completion of the user's chosen goal;
do not require every card. Distinguish a supported conditional proposal, an
unsupported affirmative claim and unfinished work. A proposed requirement
does not claim the capability or permission already exists, and earlier
conditions remain active unless later advice contradicts or changes them.
Neither a conditional requirement nor unfinished work alone establishes a
scientific error.

Materiality depends on the consequence, not whether an expected sentence
appeared. Comparable valid investigation choices and estimators are allowed.
For v11, including the supported consultant v8 releases, observe the four populated public sections,
flat A/Q/N items and optional unnumbered preceding-turn summary against the saved
typed response. Omit empty sections and redundant summaries. Read labels within
their actual reply and conversation; they do not establish record identity or
permission. Missing answers, detached qualifications or relabeled options can
matter even when other text survives. Keep earlier renderers' historical contracts.

Count each complete visible item using the [runner guide](runner.md), including
qualifications and expanded scope terms. V11 ordinary items and the optional
summary have a 50-word allowance. Explicitly requested answers or clarification
may use up to 200 words. Consultant 8.0.1, 8.0.2, 8.0.3, 8.0.4, 8.0.5, 8.0.6, 8.0.7, 8.0.8, 8.0.9, 8.0.10, 8.0.11, 8.0.12, 8.0.13, 8.0.14 and 8.0.15 require every next-step option
to stay within 50 words. The frozen observation profile retains the conditional
100-word scope allowance for 8.0.0 and historical profiles. Review the actual
request and any permitted exception's necessity; a heading, item type or model's
exemption proves neither. Presentation observations do not truncate internal
scientific evidence or requested report artifacts.

Keep presentation, content correspondence, scientific meaning, delivery provenance
and user consent as separate findings. An isolated 51-word item is a minor writing
issue; omitted qualifications or ignored withdrawal can have larger consequences.
Before attributing corruption to retained text, compare the original bytes using
the stated encoding with the relevant bound counterpart. Rendered console
appearance alone is insufficient; if the comparison cannot be made, report it
as unverified. Matching bytes do not establish scientific meaning or authorization.
Exact copied text matching an earlier message is not native identity or current
order. Missing capture evidence is unobserved, not proof that consent never occurred.
Read original incoming messages and their committed handling against actual
user meaning; do not certify a selection, stop or correction by regex. For v8,
handling can be `knowledge`, `decision` or `interpretation`. A separate
interpretation narrative is required only when other handling does not preserve
consequential meaning. The project is a generated current-state export and the
brief is a selected continuation view; inspect full journal entries when their
detail matters.
If the consultant resolves an issue from a reliable accessible source, it need
not ask the user the same question. If an issue cannot be resolved, conditional
advice can be correct. Calling an unasked resolvable issue a limitation does not
excuse a resulting wrong or unnecessarily unhelpful conclusion.

Examine whether plausible answers to the selected check could change the target,
design, interpretation or next action, and whether
the question or source could distinguish them. An easy anomaly check may be
useful, but cannot substitute for a consequential attainable assignment or timing
check. Credit a supported unchanged recommendation as well as a changed one.
Question counts, review labels and stated intentions alone establish no depth.

Complete design documentation can justify proceeding without more user
questions, but does not waive consequential checks against available data and
records. Assess whether identifiers, assignments, dates, missingness and other
relevant properties were reconciled with the documented study. Select checks
that could matter for this case; do not require every diagnostic or extra turns.
A claim that all checks passed needs supporting code, outputs or inspection
evidence. Lack of a scripted check alone does not prove that a manual inspection
was skipped: distinguish an observed defect from an unobserved check, and state
the resulting coverage limit.

Evaluate completion and credited coverage against the original objective,
actual scope choices and delivered content. An offer to prepare work is not its
delivery. Credit each planning element only to the extent established; useful
conditional advice does not by itself establish a settled decision target or
feasible resource priority. Descriptive work can be a useful intermediate
deliverable while the causal inquiry remains open. Accepting that calculation
does not by itself establish that accessible causal evidence was exhausted. An
explicit user decision to narrow or end the inquiry should be honored, with
unresolved questions retained as limitations. Apply the selected case's
completion contract and the actor's actual choices. A user pause can be valid
while the full-report objective remains incomplete; neither a target turn count
nor a recap establishes delivery.

## Actor Fidelity

Review the actor against its frozen persona and its own visible learning history,
separately from consultant quality. Check realistic knowledge and reasoning,
cooperation, semantically warranted source release, and persistence of supported
belief or decision changes. Novice-adversarial users need not use technical labels;
experts actively propose methodological alternatives within their knowledge. Neither gains
access to `world.json` or the reviewer key. Private world truth is not proof that
the actor or consultant knew it.

Assess disclosure, pending requests and learning from the actual spoken replies,
received sources and private reply history against the actor's frozen policy
and allowed knowledge. Check which narrow requests were answered and whether
other requests remain pending or were legitimately retired. Apply that actor's
item limit and its exceptions for essential qualifications or inseparable
descriptions. Check new factual updates against their supporting sources;
a consultant restatement alone does not establish a new study fact. Fact IDs,
compliance assertions and structurally valid knowledge, belief or decision
updates do not verify what was disclosed or learned. Count a reset of a
resolved misunderstanding, fabricated access, secret-password release rule,
or pressure beyond the persona's finite rule as an actor/fixture issue when
consequential. Do not punish an honest limited report because the
novice-adversarial persona initially wanted a stronger conclusion.

The standard profile represents a typical user whose initial collection context
is unvolunteered and whose model and stronger-claim questions follow the actual
discussion. Assess its two finite claim opportunities separately from motivated
pressure in `novice-adversarial`. The expert combines domain and statistical
knowledge and must actively propose at least two distinct methodological or
alternative analysis ideas before selecting the final report. Stronger-claim
pressure is not required. Resolved or untriggered optional pressure opportunities
do not justify extra exchanges.

For the expert, cite the two actual user proposals and at least one intervening
substantive user reply without a new proposal. An empty acknowledgment, two ideas
in one reply, or proposals on consecutive user turns do not satisfy the spacing.
Judge distinctness by analytical purpose, assumptions or comparison, rather than
method labels or tuning alone. A passive choice among the consultant's options
or a generic request to list methods does not establish an active proposal.
The consultant's earlier method discussion cannot retire this requirement.

Review actor coverage and the consultant's response separately. Check whether
the consultant explained the merits and limitations, requested discriminating
information, improved the idea or justified its rejection, and whether the final
analysis and report reflect the resulting decision. Neither implementation nor
agreement is required for success. Specific methods are not prescribed, and no
total turn count substitutes for these observations. An explicit user or operator
stop can leave expert coverage incomplete without an actor defect; forgetting a
proposal or selecting the final report before both proposals is an actor-fidelity
issue. Preserve the incomplete coverage when reporting consultant performance.

For coordinator 1.1.0, apply the required participation and numeric bounds in
the [bounded continuity case guide](rggvy-continuity.md); frozen 1.0.0 retains
its original criteria. Establish each required event using a distinct public
client message and consultant reply, with actual substantive deferral/return
spacing. Private actor assertions do not establish occurrence. Missing or
unproven events make stress outcome `incomplete`, even if a report is complete;
record whether an omission is observed or the evidence is unavailable, using
existing coverage reasons and findings. No new event record schema is needed.

Occurrence differs from handling. `coverage: observed` means assessable, not
passed. A publicly established preference revision can exercise the stress even
when the consultant later forgets it; retain observed coverage and its consultant
finding, which may make quality `fail`. Bounds are mechanical, while public
anchors and retained records establish participation and memory handling.
Unrelated missing scientific review evidence follows the usual quality rules
and does not automatically erase report completion or bounded participation.

Apply the actual frozen persona's exceptions, not an upstream default. Every
actor's spoken message is limited to 50 words. Matrix and RGGVY cases permit
two semantic items; the four planning cases permit one small item and follow
their frozen selection order and correction priority. Count
answered questions, confirmed/declined choices and released records or connected
bundles where the frozen packet permits them. Where the packet permits free item
choice, the actor may defer a requested blocker or correction; do not require
the convenient choice. A single label or fact card does not make several
independent answers one small item. Additional answers hidden in acknowledgments still count. Check the
spoken message, not private structured fields, against the word cap. Only an
explicitly unreliable frozen packet, such as `novice-adversarial`, permits false
spoken claims. Outside that exception, inventions about staff contacts,
unavailable logs or real-participant provenance are unsupported facts, not
ordinary uncertainty. Genuine document bytes and the
fixed source inventory remain required for every persona.

A one-turn partial or nearby answer to a compound or unclear request can be
faithful behavior without a public list of everything omitted. Check whether
`unanswered_questions` privately retains the omitted parts, whether the
consultant notices a consequential ambiguity or seeks a focused follow-up, and
whether the actor responds once its meaning is clear. A focused clarification
can also be a faithful reply when warranted by the visible wording and persona's
fluency. Do not require these difficulties in every run. Repeated evasion,
discarded pending requests, invented study facts or a false implication of
authorization remain actor defects; assess consultant recovery separately.
For a frozen `interaction_challenges` opportunity, check its visible trigger,
persona fit and recovery condition. An untriggered opportunity is not an actor
failure, and a turn target cannot justify a forced misunderstanding.

Inspect retained `actor-inputs` bundles, each reply's `actor_context`, and actual
host invocation evidence. A named context is a declaration; only retained host
evidence can establish that it did not inherit the oracle or reviewer context.
Check `action_intents` against the whole public message: a future report goal is
not a current selection. Natural early-report pressure remains a legitimate
persona behavior. Review rejected releases separately; the operator blocking an
attempt does not prove the actor was faithful, nor that outcomes leaked.
For held-out outcomes, trace `source-release-check.json` to a previously public,
saved candidate, utility, comparators and evaluation procedure. Exact text and
hash anchors establish retention and order; read them for scientific adequacy.
A missing mechanical receipt cannot justify a fictional office demand or a new
public release rule. Attribute operator handling and actor inventions separately
and retain the rejected attempt even when an existing commitment later resolves it.

Represent these judgments with the existing coverage criteria and findings,
not an extra actor-fidelity score. A material or fundamental finding owned by
`simulator`, `fixture`, `harness` or `environment` makes execution invalid for a
whole-case comparison. Keep separately supported consultant defects visible;
invalid actor behavior is not evidence that the consultant failed the same task
under a faithful user.

## Independent Checks

For `completion_contract: "full_report"`, locate the delivered consultant
report and its completed report run in the retained dossier. Verify that it
answers the requested decision, explains the study and method, reports supported
results with uncertainty and relevant diagnostics, and preserves causal limits,
alternatives and practical implications. Assess needed content rather than a
word count, prescribed headings or a visit to every specialist.

Check its selected source runs/inputs, numerical claims, reproducible companion
code/results, readback and applicable rendering evidence. A new-contract report's
manifest designates its primary, companions and assets; check each delivered format.
Historical manifests without a designation retain their original evidence.
A file named report,
a short chat recap or a report promise is insufficient. The adapter's
`completion_check` establishes artifact evidence only; scientific adequacy and
whether the report incorporates all requested work remain reviewer judgments.
Missing or unverifiable reporting makes a claimed successful full-report outcome
incomplete. Useful bounded advice may support a limited report, but cannot waive
that deliverable. Preserve environment/cap failures and attributable defects.

For `consultation-loop-v1`, inspect `consultation_loop_check` and every action's
first observed event. Compare the actual earlier assistant proposal with the
scope, then read the entire later user response. Interpret questions, corrections,
withdrawals, deferrals and changed populations before deciding it grants the
recorded action. For a source-bound report presentation adjustment, compare the requested local
formats with the actual later user message; all scientific terms stay bound to
the original offer. The observer retains attributed excerpts and the actual text;
it does not classify natural-language consent. Neither `decision_updates` nor a
candidate delivery/approval record proves agreement. A matching yes substring
does not resolve a prerequisite question or authorize a changed scope.

Check the first plan, output and transport activity, including orphan plans and
artifacts outside registered report runs. A renamed final report is still report
production. Its scope must have been offered with actual findings, diagnostics
and limits, followed by a later user selection before creation. A finding reply
may offer the report; it may not also create it using the initial request.
Later consent, an edited journal or a compliant latest state cannot erase an
earlier observed breach. The helper's assignment label does not make protected
effect/policy estimation an audit. Inspect computation contents when uncertain.

Identical bytes in a later report may come from an earlier authorized diagnostic.
The observer emits `report_output_content_role` only when the retained analysis
manifest, output bytes and observed authorization establish that ancestry. Read
the actual earlier artifact and its task before deciding its role. A hash, image
extension or analysis label cannot prove it was a diagnostic. A copied or renamed
premature report remains a violation. Unclassified ancestry remains inconclusive.

Keep structural protocol evidence, scientific findings and actor fidelity
separate. Bounded work should return useful findings and choices; a selected
extension returns to discussion, not automatic reporting. Check deferred scope
memory, partial answers, focus changes, interruption, pause/resume and reopening
where triggered. Untriggered paths are unexercised coverage, not required extra
turns. Report completion is an artifact result, not a permanently closed project.
The adapter requires a disposition for every final machine finding before
assessment can finish. Confirmed structural breaches remain failures; disputed
flags with no independently established material defect remain inconclusive.
Raw checks stay visible and missing captures remain unobserved. Per-reply snapshots
cannot establish exact within-call timing or rule out transient bypassed files;
consult actual transport/tool evidence. Diagnostic mode remains invalid and
cannot establish a passing live test.

Use `inspect --view reviewer` before writing the assessment. It saves
`review-observations.json` and binds it to the evidence index. Reconcile every
machine finding, missing-coverage entry and response discrepancy with exact
evidence references. Do not describe a previous per-turn check as the final
result. If evidence changes, regenerate the snapshot and revise the assessment.
Confirm a discrepancy separately from assigning its cause. Reassigning ownership
does not make an observed discrepancy a `false_positive`. Use `undetermined`
when invocation or other evidence cannot establish the responsible component;
do not invent a backend or transport explanation. For claims about missing
memory attribution, count the actual relevant records and quote their excerpts
against the public user messages, rather than generalizing from one empty field.
Keep report completion, observed chronology, delivery correspondence and
scientific judgment separate. Distinguish retained artifacts and prepared actor
output from delivered messages and accepted protocol events. Credit report
delivery, user acceptance and protocol stop separately from their retained
evidence; none establishes the others. State when a scientific finding concerns
an undelivered artifact. An intact reply with extra wrapper text differs from a
rewritten body. Missing exact correspondence does not by itself prove
missing consent or that report creation preceded the choice. A foreign `run_id`
on new evidence is not a revision of the run itself. Read changed conditions and
the actual public wording before drawing either conclusion.
When a candidate retains a delivery capture, compare it with the complete actual
public reply, including surrounding prose. Native capture is optional for v8 and
7.5.6. Compare assistant-recorded items, qualifications and mappings with the
independent public conversation, allowing a latest reply to await its next
appropriate resume. A matching prepared body or candidate wrapper review alone
is not an actual-reply record. Assess the actual public wording and its mapped
terms separately. Missing public/counterpart evidence leaves text correspondence
unobserved, not a proven mismatch. Separately, when the retained journal and
public sequence establish that a required actual-reply record is absent before
a dependent interpretation, report that recording omission. Distinguish it from
optional native capture and a latest reply awaiting its next appropriate resume;
do not infer a utility failure or lost public text.

Two narrowly scoped resolutions may accompany a `confirmed` machine disposition.
Keep the raw observation and cite every reference in its `adjudication_candidate`:

- For `delivery_terminal_newlines`, set `resolution` to
  `{"kind":"delivery_terminal_newlines","severity":"minor","meaning_permission_timing_unchanged":true,"review":"Explain the complete-message comparison."}`.
  Eligibility requires one retained public counterpart, matching session and
  invocation evidence, intact capture hashes and a difference limited to terminal
  CR/LF bytes. Read both complete messages before confirming that meaning,
  permission and timing are unchanged. Interior whitespace, indentation, removed
  qualifications, changed numbers or offers do not receive this exception.
- For `authorized_diagnostic_reuse`, set `resolution` to
  `{"kind":"authorized_diagnostic_reuse","content_role":"diagnostic","review":"Explain why the earlier content served the authorized analysis."}`.
  The content review must establish diagnostic use. If the earlier content was
  already a report, retain an attributable material finding with its timing and
  content evidence; do not use this resolution.

`finish` retains `consultation_loop_check` verbatim and records the supported
resolutions in `machine_finding_adjudications`, with a separate
`adjudicated_consultation_loop_status` used for scoring. A real formatting
discrepancy is still confirmed, not a false positive. Historical dispositions
without a resolution remain conservative. These resolutions cannot excuse
missing captures, unresolved message identity, source-release gaps, authorization
violations, material scientific defects or diagnostic-mode isolation limits.

Review `source_release_trace` observations against recorded releases and raw
invocations. Identical on-request source bytes appearing in a saved work snapshot
before a recorded release establish unverified acquisition. They do not prove
that the consultant used the file early or that the actor leaked it. A gated
release also needs its retained prerequisite receipt. Preserve unexplained gaps
as incomplete coverage, even when the resulting numbers appear correct.

Trace consequential recommendations and findings from their stated target
through the observed or proposed analysis population, identifying assumptions,
calculation and uncertainty interpretation. A restricted sample may still
identify the original target under justified conditions; a different target
needs its own interpretation and, if claimed causal, identification. Check the
claimed guarantee, including relevant estimated inputs, rather than treating
numerical agreement as validation. A useful conditional sensitivity scenario
need not have a confidence-coverage guarantee.
Open actual saved code and outputs for material numerical claims. Apply the
case's independent row-level or estimator/uncertainty references and declared
tolerances. Check input versions, event timing, exclusions, joins, model settings,
uncertainty units and the reported population. A run manifest proves neither
that code ran nor that the estimator or interval is correct. Use real execution
logs and, when needed, a rerun in a fresh permitted output folder, leaving the
consultant's completed run intact.

For a valid unanticipated method, review its identifying argument and compatible
numerical reference without moving the target or adding facts. If a necessary
oracle is absent, record missing coverage, not an automatic pass or failure
because it differs from the preferred method. A later fixture revision is new
evidence, not a repair to the original score.

Assess comprehension from the actual user-facing explanation: can this user
identify the comparison, finding or limitation, and next action? Actor agreement
or satisfaction alone is not evidence. Any optional blind-reader comprehension
check receives only the public exchange and audience profile, not the answer key.

Inspect substantive explanations even when the output is labelled descriptive
or non-causal. Evidence addressing a particular mechanism or setting does not by
itself establish a broader identifying assumption. Claims that adjustment
removes a mechanism, resolves confounding or makes groups causally comparable
need a supported argument using the actual sources, variable roles and timing.
A cautious headline cannot repair an unsupported explanation.
Check material unit conversions and quantitative explanations as well as fitted
coefficients, using the source and tolerance required for that claim.
Compare later recaps with the conditions supporting earlier results. An
unsupported detection threshold or a conditional claim becoming unconditional
is a scientific issue even when the main numerical estimate is correct.

For a focused rehearsal, synthetic `oracle.json` files retain short
`developer_probes.applied_interpretation` subcases. Give an independent consultant
only a subcase's `input`, then have the reviewer compare its actual response with
the private `review`. These are separate hypothetical examples, not additions to
the consultation's world. `claim_calibration` pairs an unsupported passage with a
minimal correction for `c-claim-meaning`: cite the exact claim and evidence, and
accept valid alternatives. The arithmetic self-checks do not score reasoning;
neither keywords nor a disclaimer elsewhere certify a consistent recommendation.

## Behavioral and Evidence Checks

Count at most one substantive specialist review per assistant turn, whether
local or delegated. Related diagnostics or several guides can serve one bounded
review. Two independent reviews inside one worker are still two. Clarification,
correction and synthesis may use zero. Do not infer compliance from agent names,
final prose, saved review counts or provider-reported agent-turn counts alone.
Missing trace coverage remains unobserved. Saved reviews also cannot establish
exact session continuity; that requires captured invocation/session evidence.

Use the candidate's read-only verification and captured journal/run records
for project continuity, pre-result plans, immutable completed work, input lineage
and truthful incomplete/failure states. Do not impose archived controller headings, approval
phrases, scope/receipt schemas, fixed artifact counts or a state revision on
every turn. Ordinary no-output audits need no run; saved preparation and new
target computation do. A returned user-facing answer need not make every open
question disappear or close an honestly incomplete operation.
V8 continuation is derived from direction and owned records. Do not require an
authored checkpoint, a fixed recent-intake window or new versions for unchanged
knowledge. Verify retained obligations, partial support and question remainders
through their canonical records and sources, without requiring duplicate prose.

For v8 or a retained profile reporting `user-question-routing-v1`, compare the
incoming message with its committed handling and actual work. A prerequisite
question or material ambiguity needs an answer or clarification before dependent
work. A direct answer can require zero specialist work.
Questions that depend on analysis should be recorded before work and answered
at closeout, or remain pending with a reason. An independent question may be
answered alongside clear authorized work; complexity alone does not require
clarification. Check that later context retains unresolved user questions and
that an answer does not silently change the original report objective.

V3 and v4 keep their historical heading contract. V5 may omit empty sections and
uses compact public proposals linked to the full immutable scope. Check actual
material terms and source boundaries in the reply, the retained linked scope at
offer time, and its exact execution binding. The observer deliberately preserves
the old V4 execution-field visibility test only for the old format. A complete
linked plan does not excuse hiding a consequential restriction from the user.
Quoted user questions are not new requests to the actor. Use committed handling,
structured answers, pending references and actual sources to assess this
distinction. A heading, saved answered status or matching response hash does not
establish an adequate answer or scientific validity. Earlier v1/v2 exchanges
retain their historical contract. Apply the existing continuity and usefulness
criteria without changing frozen case criteria or requiring extra rounds.

## Outcomes Without False Passes

For `verified_host`, a valid review includes `host_checks` for candidate loading,
exact resume, private isolation and role separation. Each check needs a verified
flag, an evidence-based reason and bound references to retained `private/host/`
evidence. Unsupported checks leave validity unverified or invalid as appropriate.

In `claude_explicit` mode, `candidate_loading_check` reconciles every retained
prompt, loading receipt, command, frozen entry hash and fresh/resumed session.
Missing evidence stays unverified; conflicting evidence invalidates execution.
A reviewer disposition cannot waive either into a qualified pass. A matched
check proves that the staged entry instructions were supplied, not that later
guide reads or helper executions used that candidate. Review the actual native
tool paths and explain that evidence in the candidate-loading host check, citing
relevant actual-attempt `transport/stdout.txt` alongside host evidence. This
does not establish scientific understanding or filesystem isolation. Diagnostic
mode still cannot establish a qualified pass or comparative model-performance
claim; retain supported scientific findings. Missing loading mode keeps the
historical discovery contract and does not gain explicit-loading evidence.

Keep these fields separate in the assessment:

| Field | Values and meaning |
|---|---|
| `test_validity` | `valid`, `invalid`, `unverified`: whether fixture execution, private-information boundaries and required continuity are established |
| `outcome` | `objective_met`, `useful_stop`, `incomplete`, `execution_error`: what the consultation actually achieved |
| `coverage` | Each required criterion is `observed`, `unobserved` or `not_applicable`, with evidence/reason; a counterexample is observed, not missing |
| `findings` | Defects with `owner` (`consultant`, `simulator`, `harness`, `fixture`, `environment`, `undetermined`), `severity`, `criterion`, `description`, consequence and actual `evidence_refs` |
| `quality_rating` | Derived as below, not chosen to conceal a finding or incomplete objective |
| `resources` | Actual measured work, provider coverage, limits and any breaches, distinct from scientific validity |

Use `not_applicable` only for a predeclared conditional criterion whose trigger
does not apply. Do not waive a required behavior because the consultant did not
reach it; that coverage remains `unobserved`. An early valid discovery may earn
credit while leaving a case's required later correction mechanism unexercised.

Derive the rating in this order:

1. An attributable material/fundamental consultant defect yields `fail`, even
   if a later cap prevents completion. With invalid/unverified execution, retain
   that bounded defect but do not claim a valid whole-case comparison.
2. Otherwise an invalid/unverified test, material/fundamental finding with
   undetermined cause, unobserved required criterion, resource
   breach or execution error yields `inconclusive`. So does an outcome that is
   neither `objective_met` nor a `useful_stop` satisfying the case's frozen
   stopping criteria; such a useful stop need not deliver an unattainable estimate.
3. With valid execution, required coverage, no cap breach and `objective_met`
   or a case-permitted `useful_stop`, minor consultant defects yield `weak`;
   no consultant defects yield `pass`.

A useful stop must answer what the user can conclude or do within the declared
limits. Refusal alone is insufficient. An attainable ready-analysis case cannot
pass by refusing or providing only general advice. An empty findings list does
not override missing evidence. An attempt ending at a cap before completion is
`incomplete`, not `useful_stop`.

Severity meanings: `minor` leaves the substantive decision equivalent;
`material` changes scientific support, requested utility or a meaningful user
decision; `fundamental` destroys trustworthy continuity/evidence or represents
unperformed work as completed. Simulator leaks, inconsistent worlds and transport
errors are attributed only when the evidence identifies the responsible component.
An undetermined cause does not by itself invalidate execution or erase the defect.
Retain the original failed record when a defect is corrected.

## Reporting and Comparison

Produce a compact outcome summary, evidence-linked findings, criterion coverage
and resource table, with the complete private dossier available for inspection.
The dossier binds fixture/candidate/configuration identities to every sent and
received message, disclosure, source release, run artifact and failed attempt.
Do not include secret credentials in captured configuration or reports.

Export with the runner and check the resulting package before sharing. Verify
all dispatched turns have retained raw transport and public messages, including
failed turns. A complete package has all required evidence with matching hashes;
a partial package lists missing or changed items and cannot establish complete
coverage. Preserve raw evidence even when a shorter human-readable transcript is
also supplied. The assessment and its prose must reflect the same final snapshot.
Preserve outcome, quality, test validity and finding owner/severity as separate
fields when summarizing the finalized assessment. Reconcile narrative highlights
with those findings; a useful artifact does not erase an actor-contaminated
scenario. Keep any correction distinguishable from the original evaluation.

Count user messages, consultant turns, substantive reviews, repeated/repair work,
active wall time and user-wait time separately. Report input, cached input,
output, provider time and cost only with their collection scope and semantics.
Unavailable telemetry is `null`/unknown, not zero. Avoid double-counting worker
usage already included in provider totals. Count failed attempts too.
Do not count an actor stop record as a consultant round. Report observed rounds
through verified report delivery separately from provisional maximum limits.
A typical round count needs repeated completed full-report consultations with
identified cases/model/host; focused recap runs do not establish it.

For the [catalog](problem-persona-matrix.md), evaluate the explicitly selected
configurations. The full matrix covers 12 problem/persona pairs; full-catalog
coverage adds the five standalone cases, for 17 independent attempts. RGGVY
uses only its Research Coordinator; the four planning actors are also fixed.
Review archived profiles only within their
original frozen cases; do not reinterpret historical behavior under new rules.
Compare the same problem's invariant scientific sources across personas, while
reviewing persona fidelity separately. Report target and actual replies
descriptively for ordinary pairs. Finishing near a target earns no credit, exceeding it is not a
defect, and the actor must not change behavior to approach it. An operational
cap reached before completion remains incomplete. No round target replaces
