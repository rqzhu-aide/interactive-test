# Client decision notes

These notes define a hypothetical client request for this consultation. They are not a historical study record, a statement from the paper's authors, or evidence about data collection.

The audience is a research team considering how much this state extract can support a claim that RGGVY improved rural household welfare. The primary interest is Chhattisgarh during the historical program period. The team has not yet committed to a particular outcome, comparison, estimator, weighting target, or scope of causal claim.

Use the supplied records first. No additional survey, administrative linkage, author contact, or national microdata acquisition is available within this consultation. An honest local assessment, including a bounded descriptive analysis when needed, is useful. Strong claims need an explanation of their support and limitations.

Discuss the design and a small number of consequential model choices before selecting bounded analysis. Discuss actual findings before selecting the final report. A local saved HTML report and reproducible analysis files are suitable; do not publish externally. A clear report that distinguishes demonstrated findings, assumptions, uncertainty, and unanswered questions meets the goal even when the original causal claim is not established.
